# Cost Guard version history

## v80.60 — 2026-10-20

- Watch no longer fetches optional model release dates on its startup thread when prices need a cold-cache or expired-cache refresh. Previously verified dates survive locally; the existing bounded background worker refreshes metadata without blocking the dashboard. Report enrichment, pinned CCost prices and promotion expiry remain unchanged.

## v80.59 — 2026-10-20

- Watch separates active/recent/startup rows from incremental local-day accounting, no longer scans month history or collects unrelated comparison samples, and renders `Today: Pending` until available daily history is reconciled. All running candidates survive age/row limits; archived usage, deletion retention, midnight/DST, independent native quotas and sample-based context-price warnings retain their semantics.
- V2 shares catalog topology/revisions and before/after consistency brackets across required snapshots; unstable trees alone retry. V1 discovery includes message/part row activity and conservatively probes liveness separately from payload-free revisions. Stable analysis depends on semantic identities/rates, not pricing retrieval dates. Mode scopes and both latest-100 eligibility/cutoff rules remain intact; privacy-safe stage/operation diagnostics and configurable large-history regressions/benchmarks expose actual work.

## v80.58 — 2026-10-19

- Diagnostics retains up to 64 observations per quota-window series (31.5 minutes at normal 30-second polling), bounded to 64 series per run. Sanitized percentage/reset, completion timestamp, duration, outcome and separate percentage/reset-change flags distinguish repeated successful values from failures, without reset-time jitter hiding unchanged percentages. Separate retrieval, last observed percentage change/change age, observed-unchanged age and Watch-publication timestamps never imply upstream freshness.
- Histories remain run-local and identity/revision-separated; concurrent Watches publish only the latest complete writer, explicitly identified by a random run ID. Independent Diagnostics report acquisition keeps its own history and performs fresh provider work. Normal Watch layout, polling, retries and stale expiry are unchanged. The Claude investigation documents CLI-owned fallback caching and newer structured usage reports without claiming the reported ten-minute lag is proven or introducing an unverified transport workaround.

## v80.57 — 2026-10-19

- Session/date prompt tables add Duration after Calls, including timed compactions and an overlap-safe Total. Session listings add whole-root Duration after # without another history hydration or aggregate Total row.
- Duration measures accumulated prompt execution wall-clock time, not calendar elapsed time: idle gaps are excluded and overlapping attributed work counts once. Existing lifecycle evidence governs running/terminal work; missing boundaries make the affected duration and total N/A. Reports share Watch's compact rounding/formatting; Watch timing and lifecycle behavior remain unchanged.

## v80.56 — 2026-10-15

- Watch quotas use an independent account-only scheduler, start-anchored 30-second healthy refreshes and one bounded lane per configured adapter. Provider attempts have a 20-second Watch deadline; blocked lanes cannot starve other providers or spawn replacements. Normal reports retain four workers and their existing wait budget.
- Transient failures, including after success, receive at most three 5/10/20-second recovery retries before 60-second degraded checks; durable auth/capability/format failures get no fast retries. Credential-backed retries pin failed accounts without reobserving healthy neighbors. Publication uses cached local usage, never source hydration; unchanged provider responses are new observations, cached replays are not.
- Observations older than 60 seconds are visibly stale and last-known values expire after five minutes even without another completion; resume never extends that bound. Privacy-allowlisted numeric evidence distinguishes attempts, completion, observation, publication, scheduling/worker delay and displayed freshness. Deterministic idle/active rendering tests demonstrate the 60-second end-to-end objective for whole-provider acquisition up to 20 seconds and responsive local scheduling/rendering; upstream propagation and external outages remain outside the guarantee.
