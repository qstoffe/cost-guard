# CCost zero fallback — FR000707

2026-11-14 · v81.51 · scoped presentation change, not a whole-architecture reassessment.

## Cause and boundaries

`ccost_amount` previously mapped null and unresolved zero to N/A. Application
display producers and Mini/Full startup guards independently inserted unavailable,
dash, blank-compaction or Loading amount strings. This explains the synthetic
new-prompt path; it does not prove the owner's intermittent private incident.

The shared reference-amount formatter now produces selected-unit zero while
canonical nulls, exact Decimal subtotals, completeness, price/usage reasons,
identities, sorting, quotas and billing remain unchanged. Positive known subtotals
remain preferable to fallback. Standalone published rates and native
Capacity/Remaining/Used are excluded. No acquisition, valuation, persistent cache,
provider call or source mutation was added.

Web's bounded `CostDisplay` observations overlay allowlisted display tokens only,
including both prepared currencies and paired cost help. Scope owners distinguish
source/root/prompt, whole session, configured day/account/window, selected report
period and model/publisher. Positive partial amounts and supported zero qualify;
unknown/incomplete zero retains the prior amount. Qualified corrections may
decrease. Components and Mini model/effort groups qualify independently; group
readiness is compact display metadata, not another exported cost ledger.
Mini observations live in the parent, surviving PiP reopen.

CLI Watch owns equivalent renderer-local amount observations, including account
day/window boundaries. One-shot reports remain stateless. AI Stats preserves
Loading-only noncost cards, but cost cards show numeric zero or matching retained
costs. This CCost-specific later owner requirement supersedes the earlier
all-cards Loading presentation, not Stats acquisition or arithmetic.
