within 90s: 20/2793 roots covered, `concurrent_source_change`, truthful unavailable
history. Real winners and time-to-first-verified history are **INCONCLUSIVE**, not
PASS. Live/Daily continued (224 Live frames, HTTP max0.2139s, publication operation
max0.2863s, Daily by14.0446s); these are candidate observations, not baseline-relative
latency parity or proof of the owner's earlier private chronology.

**Decision Delegate explicitly approved the narrow verification exception** allowing
delivery without real 2793-root final-winner/readiness-time proof. Prolonged private
scanning was disproportionate and could still lack a stable oracle; the FR explicitly
allows honest loading/unavailability. On Hold would unnecessarily withhold the
correctness improvement. No correctness, deterministic, version or protected-delivery
gate was waived. Exact private chronology was never claimed by the FR and needs no
additional exception.

Practical limitation: on large/churning installations Earlier can remain Loading or
Unable to verify latest sessions; prompt availability of the verified list there has
not been demonstrated. This is **not a claim of complete acceptance or cold-start
speed improvement**. No further private archive-wide scan was required.
