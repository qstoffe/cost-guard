# FR 000639 — Mini Watch: complete Earlier Sessions pressed fill, exact disclosure height delta, and dismiss Fit Height help on activation

**Owner-approved Ready Feature Request, 2026-11-09.** Permanent identity **000639**; Ready priority **000800** (the Ready queue is currently empty). **Backlog-authoring scope only:** publish this one FR and advance the permanent identity watermark from 638 to 639. Do not implement product code, run releases, change preferences, disturb live Mini Watch sessions, or modify unrelated backlog entries in this publication.

## User-observed problems

Three small but conspicuous issues remain in the **Web Beta → Mini Watch** Document Picture-in-Picture window after the recent header and fit-height work:

1. **Earlier Sessions selected fill has a bottom strip.** When the Earlier Sessions button is pressed/expanded, most of its interior uses the active fill, but a few pixels along its bottom appear not to be filled with the same color. The result looks like an incomplete selected state.
2. **Earlier Sessions toggling changes window height by the wrong amount.** After manually clicking **↕ Fit Mini Watch height** to get a snug current window, expanding and then collapsing **Earlier Sessions** can leave a different outer window height. Clicking Fit Height once more makes the window shrink further. The disclosure should add/remove **only the exact actual height contributed by the Earlier Sessions section**, not refit the entire Mini contents or introduce an extra reserve/margin.
3. **Fit Height tooltip remains visible after clicking.** The tooltip on the **↕ Fit Mini Watch height** button should disappear **immediately on activation**, rather than remaining over the window after the action has been performed.

These are three parts of one narrow **Mini Watch interaction/layout correction**; they should be fixed and verified together without a header redesign.

## Relevant source-level findings (candidates, not claims of proven rendered root cause)

- **`src/web/assets/mini.css`**: `.mini-header .mini-earlier-toggle[aria-pressed="true"]` sets the pressed background to `var(--accent)`, but also applies **`box-shadow: inset 0 -2px 0 var(--accent-ink)`**. That explicitly paints a contrasting ~2 px stripe inside the bottom edge and is a strong first candidate for the reported visual defect. Check actual computed styles, theme state and screenshots rather than assuming this is the only cause.
- **`src/web/assets/mini.js::DisclosureSize.toggle()`** currently toggles the disclosure, then uses **`view.fitHeight()`** (the whole-window opening/disclosure target, including the two-running-prompt-row reserve), with a different collapse branch for `fit-on-open` versus `remember-last`. **`DisclosureSize.fit()`**, however, deliberately uses **`view.fitHeight(false)`** to fit only the content actually present. This discrepancy can explain why the expand/collapse round trip does not preserve the result of a preceding explicit Fit Height click. The issue is the **delta contract**, not a request to make disclosure invoke Fit.
- **`mini.js::View`** registers Fit Height's help with **`ContextHelp.attach(this.fitButton, ..., true)`**. In **`src/web/assets/help.js`**, that third argument makes a trigger **passive**, so clicking can pin/show help instead of dismissing action help. Fit Height is an actionable control, not a passive help-only target; use the shared action-help semantics or equivalent scoped immediate dismissal.
- Keep the existing **`DisclosureSize`** native sizing owner, `ContextHelp` ownership, existing responsive header, `aria-expanded`/`aria-pressed`, visual themes and access semantics. Prior relevant deliveries are **FR000635** (Mini header/filled Earlier state), **FR000637** (manual ↕ Fit Height) and the existing Earlier disclosure/size work.

## A. Fix the Earlier Sessions filled-state pixels

1. When collapsed, **Earlier Sessions** remains an unfilled/outlined button. When expanded, its **entire intended interior fill reaches the bottom inner boundary uniformly**, without a contrasting unfilled/underfilled horizontal strip, accidental inner inset, border gap or cut-off at the bottom. Remove or revise any problematic pressed-state inset shadow; keep the visible normal button boundary and its corner radius intentional.
2. Keep the current single button, unchanged **Earlier Sessions** text, no arrow, and existing coherent alignment with the title, ↕ action and CCost Today. Hover, pointer down, keyboard focus and touch should not replace the active fill with a partially painted state or leave a residual band. Use the existing semantic theme colors with readable contrast in **Classic and Web light/dark**, and maintain an appropriate forced-colors/focus indication.
3. Preserve accessibility and behavior: `aria-pressed="true"` and `aria-expanded="true"` only when the table is expanded, both false when collapsed; no extra toggle indicator, status row, vertical spacer or change to row data.

## B. Earlier Sessions must add/remove exactly its own rendered height delta

1. The **Earlier Sessions disclosure click is a relative height adjustment**, not an implicit Fit Height operation. At the window's **current actual content width**, determine the positive visible-layout delta

   **ΔEarlier = rendered Mini content height with Earlier expanded − rendered Mini content height with Earlier collapsed**,

   holding all non-Earlier visible content, dimensions, fonts, zoom and state constant. This accounts for the **actual Earlier table/header/rows/empty state and any section margin, gaps, padding, borders or layout reflow caused by disclosure**. Hidden Earlier content contributes zero when collapsed. It must **not** add the entire Mini content height, a historical/cached target, two synthetic running-prompt rows, an unrelated footer/blank margin or a fixed arbitrary safety reserve.
2. **On expand:** request a vertical window-height increase of **exactly ΔEarlier**, preserving the user's immediately preceding height baseline (whether fit by ↕, opened by Fit-on-open, remembered, or manually dragged). **On collapse:** remove exactly the achieved expansion contribution and return to the preceding baseline when other layout/native geometry has not changed. The collapse must **not** refit or shrink unrelated visible content.
3. **Critical reproduction/acceptance sequence:** open Mini with Earlier collapsed; click **↕ Fit Mini Watch height**; record outer height **H0** and full rendered layout; click Earlier to expand; record **H1**; click Earlier again to collapse; record **H2**. With stable content and a browser that accepts the resize, **H1 − H0 = ΔEarlier** and **H2 = H0**, at actual available native-pixel/DIP precision. Pressing Fit Height afterward must **not** produce the extra shrink currently observed. Repeated expand/collapse cycles must never accumulate one or several pixels of height drift.
4. Test the same delta-only round trip starting with a manually taller or shorter window, with **Mini Watch size = Fit on open** and **Remember last size**, with no/one/multiple running rows, zero/one/three Earlier rows and empty/loading states, account cards, visible messages, narrow width, genuine wrapping, different fonts/themes and zoom/DPR. Do not silently overwrite the user's chosen baseline or reinstate the opening-only two-row reserve after the user has explicitly fitted to real content.
5. Measure both disclosure states at **the same effective current width**, respecting CSS zoom and native device-pixel rounding. Use a stable inert/hidden measurement or equivalent that does not expose an intermediate UI state, shift focus, trigger source work or alter scroll position. Keep the current single native resize owner and **at most one deliberate native height request per eligible user click**; no timer, repeated correction loop, passive Live/SSE resize or width-fitting/reposition request.
6. **Native limitations are real:** OS/Chromium can quantize dimensions, change achieved width on a nominal height-only resize, clamp at work-area/minimum/maximum height, or refuse resizing. Observe **achieved** bounds; restore the pre-expansion baseline when feasible, avoid falsely claiming exact native geometry when it is impossible, and allow normal vertical scrolling instead of clipping rows. Manual resizing or changed content **between** toggles must invalidate stale restoration assumptions and recompute or reconcile the appropriate current delta deterministically, without an extra global Fit. A source update alone never triggers a resize.

## C. Immediately dismiss the ↕ Fit Mini Watch height tooltip on click

1. Clicking/tapping **↕ Fit Mini Watch height**, or activating it with Enter/Space, **closes any currently visible/pending help for that exact control immediately** in the same activation, whether the sizing succeeds, is already fitted, is clamped or is refused. The popup must not remain pinned/cover content after the action. Neither a held pointer over the button nor persistent keyboard focus should instantly reopen it from the same action.
2. Preserve useful tooltip discovery on **a new deliberate hover/focus/touch interaction**, its explanatory wording and the accessible name **Fit Mini Watch height**. Use the existing shared `ContextHelp` action-dismiss/suppression behavior; specifically investigate whether passing `true` (passive) to `attach()` is the reason a click pins it. Do not globally change passive help behavior for other value/heading triggers or remove the button's keyboard functionality.
3. No added tooltip close icon, new popover system, focus theft, additional visible message or persistent tooltip state.

## Acceptance and verification

- **Pressed fill:** real DOM computed styles and cropped screenshots of Earlier Sessions in collapsed/expanded/hover/focus states show no bottom-edge 1–3 px contrasting band; repeat in Classic/Web light/dark and at 100%, 125%, 150% and 200% zoom, where feasible, and check forced-colors/focus. Confirm both ARIA states and table visibility.
- **Pixel-accurate delta:** capture the Earlier section and whole content `getBoundingClientRect()` before/after each disclosure together with requested/achieved PiP `outerHeight`, `innerHeight`, width, zoom and DPR. In ordinary unclamped cases assert growth equals the **measured rendered ΔEarlier**, exact closed baseline restoration and no extra change on subsequent manual Fit. Distinguish unavoidable browser quantization from application padding/target drift.
- **Sizing behavior:** cover Fit-before-expand, Fit-after-collapse, repeated cycles, initially oversized/undersized/manual-size windows, both size preferences, unchanged vs changed history/active rows, width wrapping, native min/max/refusal and manual-intervening resize. No document/content clipping, hidden overflow, unexpected horizontal growth requested by the app or passive source-driven resize.
- **Help lifecycle:** open the Fit Height tooltip by hover/focus, then activate by pointer and keyboard; verify it is dismissed **synchronously**, does not reopen under a stationary pointer/focus, and becomes discoverable on a new interaction. Include no-op/already-fit and native-refusal cases. The Earlier toggle and other Web help retain their current behavior.
- Extend focused **`development/tests/fixtures/mini_disclosure.cjs`**, **`development/tests/integration/probe_mini_disclosure.cjs`**, **`probe_mini_fit.cjs`** and relevant Mini width/help probes; verify in real Chromium/Edge Document PiP rather than treating a synthetic CSS/unit test as complete browser proof. Update **`docs/web-mini-watch.md`** when implementing, not during FR publication.
