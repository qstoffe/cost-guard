# FR 000685 — AI Stats: replace Token Consumption Rate with a Top 3 provider-response tokens/sec podium

**Owner-approved Ready to Develop FR, 2026-11-12; owner clarification added 2026-11-12.** Permanent ID **000685**, temporary Ready priority **001030**. Repurpose **Token Consumption Rate** into a Top 3 model TPS podium. **Equally binding: restore and permanently document the AI Stats-wide fun-first, BEST-EFFORT, NEVER-UNAVAILABLE display policy.** Preserve the formulas and behavior of every existing card other than the replaced token-rate card, except correcting any prohibited unavailable placeholder.

## Non-negotiable, permanent AI Stats policy: BEST EFFORT — NO "Unavailable"

**Owner clarification overriding every stricter interpretation below, in FR000682, or in earlier conservative report conventions:** AI Stats is a deliberately playful best-effort statistics page, **not a trustworthy-accounting dashboard or scientific benchmark**. This instruction applies to **all present and future AI Stats cards**, not merely the new podium. The owner has repeatedly removed `Unavailable` from these cards; it must **not be reintroduced** whenever an estimate or imperfect historical evidence is involved.

- **While an actual selected-period acquisition is underway:** the already-established card shells contain only plain **`Loading…`**. Do not reveal provisional normal-looking values as if the period finished; reuse ready matching-period last-good results during quiet refresh.
- **After a successful ready result:** show a **number, ranking, fun fact, sensible estimate or genuinely empty-period playful value**. **Never display the text `Unavailable`, `N/A`, an empty quality-gate placeholder, a missing-coverage badge or a hidden value solely because evidence is incomplete, approximated, sparse, volatile, partly unpriced, from an old source, or falls short of a high-confidence standard.** Prefer a useful estimate based on the real observations over suppressing the card. One bad or undated historical request cannot poison the entire period or another model's podium.
- **A few real observations are enough for best-effort sampling.** A single plausibly timed model call can appear on the podium with `1 sampled call`. Do not impose arbitrary minimum sample size, complete-catalog/period coverage, independent proof of every event boundary, a ‘high confidence’ flag or perfect source completeness as a prerequisite for displaying a fun statistic. Keep scores roughly plausible; impossible timestamps/zero elapsed time are not valid arithmetic denominators, but imperfect intervals may be **approximate**.
- **Genuine lack of activity:** a completed truly empty period can show zero-style fun cards or a friendly **“The podium is waiting for its first race.”** That is not a missing-data quality gate. If measured period activity exists but a given metric has limited evidence, use a sensible sample/estimate, not an empty-state claim that no activity occurred.
- **Real operational failure is different from low quality:** do not invent usage, claim a failed fetch completed, or run `Loading…` forever after an unrecoverable failure. Preserve automatic recovery, matching last-good data and a truthful **page-level operational error** if absolutely necessary, without labelling individual Stats cards `Unavailable`. The ban is on conservative statistic-value fallbacks, not on reporting genuine broken operation.
- **Hard feasibility gate before shipping this new kind of statistic:** If the existing supported sources provide **no defensible per-model provider-call duration proxy at all**, explicitly **do not implement / do not mark this TPS feature complete**; report the blocker for owner discussion. **Never ship a completed podium that says “Unavailable” or “Not enough reliable evidence” by design.** If the feature is implementable from approximate but sensible response timings, **ship it as best effort** and keep normal values visible; do not recreate the old rejection loop.

**Make this policy hard to forget in future changes:** During implementation, add a short unmistakable **developer-facing policy comment** beside `POLICY`/`StatsObservation` in `src/analysis/ai_stats.py` and at the top of `src/web/assets/ai-stats.js`. Add a stable, explicit **"AI Stats = best effort / never Unavailable"** section to the owning architecture documentation and a brief user-facing description; link those from relevant tests. Add **regression assertions covering every ready AI Stats card across empty, sparse, partial, estimated and real-source samples**: no quality-gated `Unavailable`/`N/A` text, while loading and real operational errors remain distinct. This is a standing design rule for future additions, not one-off copy polishing.

- Show at most **three model rows**, highest **generated output tokens per second** first, with **🥇 gold, 🥈 silver, 🥉 bronze**. One distinct model per podium position, not three individual high-scoring prompts. Display model identity/name, readable `tokens/s` (or `tok/s`) and a modest sample count such as `24 model calls`, with terse helpful hover/focus copy. The numeric values and ranking must come from that user's observed calls, never an external benchmark or an advertised model specification.
- **Remove** the card's existing `Calendar average`, `Workday average` and `Active AI average` rows and all associated hourly-token-help text and “Unavailable” treatment driven by whole-prompt active time. Do not turn the new card into a fourth rate table elsewhere. Keep the card in its current Overview position/layout, with the familiar playful tone and existing responsive styling.
- Keep the current **Loading-only** card-shell presentation while the selected-period acquisition actually runs, and same-period last-good retention. After readiness, show the complete sampled podium atomically. For a genuinely empty selected period, use a small friendly zero-races line; **do not mislabel sparse but real model activity as empty or hide the entire podium behind a quality gate**. There must be **no `Unavailable` / `N/A` in any ready AI Stats card**.

## Precise metric: provider-response throughput, not prompt-workflow speed

The owner means the elapsed time **from a specific model call being sent to its provider until the provider response is received/finished**. This is a client-observed **provider request round-trip / response throughput** metric: it includes upstream queueing, network transit, time-to-first-token, model generation and response delivery, insofar as they occur inside that call. It is **not** a pure GPU decoding benchmark and should not be labelled as such.

**Crucially exclude** all *unrelated local harness work*: time before dispatch while the client prepares context, local tool invocations, filesystem/terminal actions, agent orchestration between calls, prompt-wide waits, human idle time and post-response local processing. A prompt can execute multiple sequential model requests separated by tools; each eligible request must have **its own numerator and its own provider-call interval**. Background/subagent requests may qualify individually for their actual model, but the enclosing parent prompt's elapsed time never becomes their denominator.

Per eligible provider call:

`response_tps ≈ observed generated output tokens / ((observed provider-call finish - observed provider-call start) / 1000)`

For one displayed model over the selected period use the volume-weighted aggregate:

`sampled_model_tps ≈ SUM(sampled call output tokens) / SUM(sampled call provider-response durations in seconds)`

Do **not** average the per-call rounded TPS values or select a single unusually fast outlier as the model's period speed. Use unrounded numerator/denominator for sorting, format a compact numeric value only at presentation. **The result is a fun sampled estimate, not a guaranteed exact speed**; the model's observed request interval is the best-effort approximation to provider response time.

**Numerator:** provider-observed **generated output** tokens for that exact model request (including separately reported reasoning generation if and only if established canonical output semantics include it without double-counting). **Never** use Input, cached input, cache write, or Total Tokens (which is traffic across all categories) as the TPS numerator. Preserve per-request source/provider/model/variant/step identity and existing step-over-message/tool-continuation/clone deduplication. Attribution of usage and timing must agree; do not attach a parent duration to a child model or merge different provider responses into one.

**Period membership:** align with the selected AI Stats period's existing request completion-time convention, current timezone/day boundaries and source generation, including the already supported All time and archived roots when available. **Sample from** independently attributable completed calls; one long call belongs to its finishing period (do not prorate time or tokens through calendar boundaries). Do not process or rehydrate every model call again merely to build this card.

**Model identity:** rank unique actual provider/model identities. If variants/effort settings have materially different response behavior, either keep them distinct and clearly label them or aggregate with a documented consistent rule that does not silently conflate incompatible identities. Deterministic ties and zero/one/two eligible models are handled cleanly; avoid fuzzy provider aliases. The goal is a Top 3 of *used models*, not the top three requests or externally benchmarked speeds.

## Required bounded sampling per model (best effort, efficient)

The owner explicitly **does not require scanning every prompt/model invocation or perfect speed measurements**. A smart, limited sample per actual model is preferable to slow/full-history precision:

1. **Per-model budget:** start with a modest, clearly documented cap such as **up to 32 representative completed calls per distinct provider/model per selected period** (implementer can tune a similar practical bound after profiling). **It is a maximum, not a minimum**: 1–2 available calls should be displayed rather than rejected. Avoid a single global 32-call sample that starves less-used models. Derive it from **already-read compact source evidence** or the existing bounded AI Stats worker; no extra exhaustive historical walk.
2. **Stable fair selection:** choose reproducibly using actual existing per-call identities, preferably a mix of reasonably recent and period-spread calls so one extraordinary invocation or a single burst does not dominate. Document exact deterministic sampling/reservoir/strata rule and how retained/revised roots invalidate affected samples; avoid random reranking on every visit. Keep effort/variant attribution sensible.
3. **Amortized cost:** cap per-model retained timing facts and expensive normalization. Use existing 256-root slices, compact evidence/cache and source-revision keys; update incrementally when roots change. Normal period switches may reuse the same sampled facts. If the existing first cold acquisition is extensive, **do not add another full-history pass** or block other ready cards while doing per-model sampling.
4. **Sample output:** show `tok/s` based on aggregate sampled output/elapsed time plus **actual sampled model-call count**, e.g. `17 sampled calls`; short title help explains approximate **provider call round-trip** timing including network/TTFT, not pure generation decoding. No quality/confidence badges, threshold badges or unavailable disclaimers. Small samples and imperfect-but-plausible timestamps are accepted.
5. **No invented denominator:** use the closest **plausible request-level** timing pair available, even if it is an approximate assistant/step proxy. Discard an individual clearly nonsensical or zero/negative duration rather than fabricating seconds or suppressing other models. **Do not require proof-quality timestamp provenance** or a perfect coverage ratio to show a reasonable ranking. Never use total prompt/workflow active time as the denominator.

## Source feasibility — important existing evidence and required resolution

FR000682 originally proposed a generation-only TPS fact but **omitted it** after a small real-source inventory found no proven **first-to-last-generated-token** interval. This FR intentionally asks for a **different, broader and potentially more feasible interval**: **dispatch-to-completion of one provider request**, including prefill/TTFT, *not* first-token-to-last-token decoding speed. Do not reject this request simply because the older first/last-output-token test failed.

Current Cost Guard relevant facts:

- `src/sources/opencode_v2_normalization.py` already normalizes assistant `time.created` and `time.completed`, optional `time.streamed`, and per-step `step-finish` usage into `ModelInvocation`. `src/domain/models.py::ModelInvocation` carries per-invocation created/completed timestamps. **Presence of fields does not yet prove they exactly bracket network send and final provider response**.
- `src/reports/ai_stats_evidence.py` currently stores prompt/compaction **execution-duration intervals** for AI Working Time; these include tool/agent workflow and are categorically **not** eligible provider-call denominators. `src/analysis/ai_stats.py::_token_metrics` currently divides total token traffic by unioned whole-workflow duration: delete/replace this token-rate use, not the shared duration or the existing cost-rate calculation.
- Current OpenCode V2 source/event vocabulary includes step start/streamed/completed; check **the actually supported installed V2/V1 versions** and their persistent event semantics. Investigate whether `step-start`, assistant `time.created`, step-finish `time.end`/assistant `time.completed` provide an **approximately useful request start-to-finish** proxy. **Do not demand laboratory-grade proof** as a veto; explain likely local overhead/bias briefly, and use a reasonable proxy when available. Beware tool-only continuations, zero-duration bursts and duplicate steps that might yield absurd six-digit `tokens/s` winners.
- Prefer the closest existing, already-acquired per-call timestamp pair as a **best-effort provider-response-duration proxy**. A transparent approximate call-level throughput **is the target behavior**, even when metadata timestamps include small unavoidable client/transport overhead. **Whole-prompt time is not an acceptable substitute.** If a narrower observable seam already exists, favor it; but do not require a new upstream timing system, perfect end-point confirmation or an arbitrary quality score before using a sensible request-level approximation. The help can say 'Approximate output tokens per second across sampled model responses.'
- If legacy V1, an older OpenCode release, or a particular provider lacks a plausible per-call time proxy, **sample whatever real calls can be approximated**; exclude only impossible individual denominators, not an entire model/period because some calls are incomplete. A period with genuinely no observed calls can show a playful empty podium. **Do not mask an actual nonempty period as empty merely because a conservative eligibility policy refused its calls.** If no supported version can produce a useful approximation at all, pause/reject the proposed podium implementation for owner decision **before claiming feature completion**, as the policy above requires.
- Before adopting a new observer/collector, first exhaust the existing normalized retained data and compact checkpoint/read-only source route. If truly necessary to support *future* observations, assess a **small supported, read-only, privacy-preserving per-call timing hook** at a currently allowed OpenCode integration boundary. It must not mutate the shared running OpenCode service, install a third-party plugin, intercept secrets, send paid model calls, or change another repository under this FR. Any required upstream change outside Cost Guard's authorized scope is a **clearly identified prerequisite**, never silently delivered or fabricated in this task. Keep older periods honest if such evidence could not have been recorded historically.

Retain the title **Token Consumption Rate** as requested, but its help must make it immediately clear that it now represents **model output response throughput (`tok/s`)**, not the total I/C/W/O consumption of the Total Tokens card or calendar/workday activity per hour.

Token Consumption Rate

Keep text selectable, headings accessible and hover/focus/touch title help concise; no new visible question-mark clutter or extra tables. **No quality badges, no `Unavailable`, no `N/A` and no withheld rank because the sample is small**; the sample count and brief help already disclose the estimates. Model calls with logically impossible denominator cannot contribute, but they must not poison other model stats.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Visual contract:** On every AI Stats period, the previous token-rate three-hour rows are gone, replaced in the same Overview card by up to three ordered medal/model/`tok/s`/sample rows. AI Hourly Rate and AI Working Time remain **exactly as they were**. Total Tokens retains its source-based I/C/W/O+reasoning traffic **calculation** (fix any forbidden unavailable display). Other Fun Facts/Overview stats and conditional matched fact keep their existing calculation. **Every ready AI Stats card must obey the permanent best-effort/no-unavailable policy.**
2. **Best-effort timing semantics:** Trace a typical supported V2 model request from its closest request start/end timestamps using **real code/schema evidence** (and bounded read-only observation if available). Distinguish its approximate **provider response round trip** from a long prompt/tool loop. Explain known proxy bias without demanding perfect proof or using start-of-first-token decoding as a false proxy. The approximate statistic must actually display for plausible observed calls even when incomplete source history prevents a 'verified' score.
3. **Independent oracle and sampling:** Multiple calls per model, different output counts/durations, parallel/overlapping models, context-cache-heavy requests, hidden reasoning, retries, aborted/incomplete and duplicate/zero-usage continuation records give correct **SUM(sampled output)/SUM(sampled per-call elapsed seconds)** and ordering. Validate deterministic, per-model bounded samples with **1/2/32/many calls**, outliers, cache reuse and revision changes. Omit objectively invalid zero/negative or unpaired intervals, **not** a whole model because of subjective confidence, small sample, a second bad call or a single missing historic root.
4. **Period and identity:** Scope switching, repeated roots and subagents, same model at different efforts, source revisions, missing old histories and cached results never leak another period's sample or count the same physical call twice. Validate exact model/source identity, deterministic tie rules, 0–3 eligible ranked models, and sample-coverage help.
5. **Loading/performance:** Reuse current AI Stats authoritative period-ready shell, safe last-good retention, checkpointed 256-root slices/64-root batches and existing host-owned data demand. **Require a capped sample per model** and no extra full-history scan/separate paid probe, no UI stall just to resolve this fun metric and no additional background loop. Warm/changed rerenders preserve focus and title help. Cold pending shows **Loading… only**, successful ready shows figures/podium even for sparse observations, genuinely empty periods show fun zero/empty facts. A real failed acquisition produces a truthful operational message, not a card-level `Unavailable`. Measure representative cold/warm and five-period payload impact.
6. **Permanent best-effort policy:** Demonstrate even **one plausibly timed provider response** can produce a nonzero sampled podium entry; data gaps, missing price, old roots and incomplete coverage must not hide existing usable responses or poison other cards. Assert no `Unavailable`/`N/A` in **any successfully ready AI Stats card**, not only this new one; preserve truthful transient Loading and separate genuine operational failures. If there is **no plausible request-level timing basis at all** for the proposed provider TPS stat, **do not implement or mark it complete**; report and discuss the blocker. Never substitute whole-workflow hours or external benchmark values.
7. **Regression / lasting policy:** Update `src/analysis/ai_stats.py`, `src/reports/ai_stats_evidence.py`, appropriate source/analysis identity adapters if justified, `src/application/{ai_stats,projection}.py`, `src/web/assets/ai-stats.js` and focused CSS; evolve policy/result validation, compact evidence cache dependencies and existing `test_ai_stats_tokens.py` / `test_ai_stats.py` / resume/browsers/fixtures safely. **Mandatory prominent source comments** on the AI Stats best-effort/no-unavailable rule in Python **and** JS plus a permanent architecture contract and browser/Python tests for every card. Preserve Python/JS layer boundaries, source identity, automatic recovery, units/themes and accessibility; update `docs/web-beta.md` and scoped AI Stats journal truthfully.

**Non-goals:** altering AI Hourly Rate, replacing Total Tokens, a global tokens/sec dashboard or new benchmark source, model speed inferred from entire prompt execution, time excluding TTFT (pure decoding TPS), new per-period Calendar/Workday rates, provider or account writes, credential collection, experimental paid inference, automatically changing OpenCode/another repository, or hiding genuine missing provenance behind fabricated highscore rows.

**Done means:** Token Consumption Rate shows an efficient **per-model sampled, approximate Top 3 provider-response output `tok/s`** podium across the selected period; **AI Hourly Rate and all other calculations remain intact**. **Every successfully loaded AI Stats card follows the documented, code-commented best-effort/no-unavailable rule.** Any total lack of usable provider-call timing is a **feature feasibility blocker to report before implementation**, not permission to ship a perpetually unavailable statistic.
