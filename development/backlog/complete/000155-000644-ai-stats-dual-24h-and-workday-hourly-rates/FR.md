# FR 000644 — AI Stats: 24h, workday, and hypothetical 8-hour robot cost on one card

**Owner-approved Ready Feature Request, 2026-11-09; clarified later the same day.** Permanent ID **000644**, Ready priority **000810** (after FR000643). This revision **updates the existing approved Ready FR in place**; it does not issue a second FR, advance the identity highWatermark beyond **644**, implement code, change settings, release, or touch unrelated backlog items.

## Owner request and intended experience

Keep **Cost Guard Web → AI Stats** as ten playful emoji cards with concise, funny tooltips and trait lines. The **first Overview card** should show **three distinct, clearly labeled numbers on the same card** (not three cards or an eleventh card):

1. **24h average — CCost/h:** the selected-period `Total AI Cost` spread across **24 nominal hours for every selected calendar date**. This is a normalization of the period's actual best-effort reference CCost, not active AI run time.
2. **Workday average — CCost/h:** that **same period total** spread across **eight nominal work hours per eligible workday** (excluding weekends and configured Swedish public holidays). This spreads **all** period spending, including weekend/night use, over conventional scheduled work hours; it does not filter cost events by when work occurred.
3. **Robot · 8h shift — CCost per hypothetical day:** take the **original AI cost per actively running AI hour** and multiply by **eight hours**. It represents what **one full robot work shift** *would cost* if AI kept running at the selected period's observed active-hour cost intensity, **not what the period really cost per workday**. In the tooltip, explain the original active-hour rate and optionally extrapolate a total for all eligible workdays in the selected period.

**Synthetic example:** seven days, five workdays, **840 CCost Credits**, and **28 hours of actually running AI**. Show:

```text
💸 AI Hourly Rate
24h average       5 CCost/h
Workday average  21 CCost/h
Robot · 8h shift 240 CCost/8h day
Standard-issue silicon. Surprisingly functional.
```

The same three values in USD are **$0.05/h**, **$0.21/h**, and **$2.40/8h day**, respectively. The third comes from **840 / 28 = 30 Credits/active hour**, then **30 × 8 = 240 Credits/robot day**. Five such hypothetical workdays would cost **1,200 Credits**; keep that full-period extrapolation in tooltip help rather than adding a fourth dominant number.

**Presentation recommendation:** one coherent card with three compact label/value rows and the existing fun trait; lightly adjust spacing/font hierarchy only for that card. Keep the familiar title and emoji. Make the fictional robot scenario visibly distinct through its **label and per-day unit**, not a warning, badge, fourth card or technical footnote.

## Important verified clarification: preserve the old active-hour value

The current `src/analysis/ai_stats.py::_cost_metrics()` calculates **`rate = total * HOUR_MS / duration`**, where `duration` is the **union of real/best-effort AI execution intervals** also used by `AI Working Time`. That existing AI Hourly Rate is **cost per actively running AI hour**; it is **not** a 24h calendar rate.

The earlier two-value version of this FR proposed replacing the original active-hour rate. **This owner clarification supersedes that:** retain the original calculation as the basis for the hypothetical robot shift. The three displayed perspectives are **24h normalized actual-period spending**, **workday-hour normalized actual-period spending**, and **fictional cost of one eight-hour day of continuous active AI at the old active-hour rate**. Never relabel the old active-hour rate as 24h, nor confuse the robot shift with `Workday average × 8`.

## A. Three rates, two time concepts, one cost numerator

1. Let **C** be the selected period's existing raw best-effort **`Total AI Cost`** in canonical reference Credits; **D** be eligible selected local calendar dates; **W** be workdays among those dates; and **A** be the selected period's raw **AI Working Time in hours** (the existing unioned active AI execution duration, including current bounded fun-first interval recovery). All three perspectives use the **same C**; no new history scan, provider quota lookup or pricing valuation.
2. **24h average** = `C / (D × 24)` Credits/h. Divide by nominal calendar days, not elapsed partial Today clock hours, nor unioned active execution hours.
3. **Workday average** = `C / (W × 8)` Credits/h. Divide by eight scheduled hours on each eligible date. This **does not exclude** cost events on non-workdays, nor imply the human was at work.
4. **Robot · 8h shift** = `(C / A) × 8` Credits **per hypothetical full eight-hour AI-running day**. **C/A** is the original existing active-hour rate; it must remain available as raw calculated data for this scenario and accessible help. **Do not calculate Robot from `Workday average × 8`**. Optional **hypothetical selected-period Robot total** = `(C / A) × (W × 8)` Credits if a robot runs **eight active hours on each of W eligible days**, shown only in tooltip rather than as another headline value.
5. **A is actual/best-effort running AI time, not scheduled human hours.** Respect the preexisting union of overlapping execution intervals, incomplete-time fallback and period clipping. A robot hypothetically running eight hours continuously may have a completely different cost from what was actually spent during 40 scheduled hours. Keep `AI Working Time`, `Total AI Cost`, and the other nine metric calculations unchanged.
6. Reuse configured **`timezone`** for dates and **`workdayCalendar`** for W: existing `src/analysis/quota_pace.py::is_workday()` excludes weekends and optional `SE` public holidays from `src/analysis/timezones.py::swedish_public_holidays()`. The shipped default is `Europe/Stockholm` and `SE`. Empty calendar means weekends only; holidays on weekends are not subtracted twice. **Do not invent vacation days, employer shutdowns or bridge days** absent a configured calendar.
7. Compute with **full Decimal precision**. Convert only the final values to the Web-selected Credits or USD with the existing `ccost_amount()` convention (**100 Credits = $1 reference USD**); retain standard **$0.00** two-decimal formatting. The first two values end in **`/h`**, the third in **`/8h day`**, **`/robot day`**, or another clearly per-day unit. Currency switching updates all three from stable raw source-prepared data without fetching.
8. **Edge cases:** if C=0, show zero-style fun numbers where denominators exist; never show NaN/Infinity. If **W=0**, Workday reads **`No workdays`**, not a fabricated nonzero monetary rate; Robot can still show the cost of **one hypothetical shift** when A>0, but its selected-period total covers **zero shifts**. If **A=0**, reuse the existing sensible AI Stats bounded duration estimate where supported, otherwise use a gentle neutral/zero-style Robot result without pretending a valid measured active-hour rate. One broken denominator must not erase the other two working figures.
9. Retain the `hourly-rate` metric identity and, preferably, its original **active-hour raw `value`** for compatibility/trait selection. Add separate explicitly named raw and display tokens for **24h**, **Workday**, **Robot day**, and optional **active-hour basis/period Robot total**. The browser must never mistake the old raw `value` for the new 24h average or compute these ratios locally. Keep the original lighthearted trait, adjusting only if necessary to remain varied.

## B. Exactly which dates count for each current AI Stats selector

Keep the **five existing selectors, their cost-source windows, current report acquisition/caching and other metrics** unchanged:

- **Today**: the **configured-local current date**, counted as **24 calendar hours** and **8 workday hours if eligible**, otherwise zero workday hours. This remains the current AI Stats Today cost numerator; do not divide merely by the hours elapsed since midnight.
- **7 days**: the existing rolling **7 × 24-hour** AI Stats cost window. For this **normalized display denominator**, use **seven local date slots ending on the current configured-local date**, not eight different dates merely because the rolling instant-to-instant window intersects partial days at both ends: **168 calendar hours** and **8 × number of eligible dates among those seven** workday hours. This local-date convention should be explicit in the tooltip if useful. Do not change the underlying `now − 7 × DAY_MS` membership/cost query to make the denominator easier.
- **30 days**: analogous existing rolling **30 × 24-hour** cost window; denominator is **720 calendar hours** and eight times the number of eligible dates among the **30 configured-local dates ending today**. Do not count an accidental 31st partial boundary date.
- **This month**: from the **first configured-local calendar date of the current month through today inclusive**, not through future dates in the month. For example, on the ninth calendar day it has **9 × 24 nominal calendar hours**, while Workday uses only workdays among those nine dates × 8. No fictitious remaining days of the month in either denominator.
- **All time**: begin on the **earliest supported local calendar date of activity/request evidence included in that All time AI Stats calculation**, and end on the current configured-local date inclusive. Count all intervening calendar dates and eligible workdays, **not merely dates on which a session ran**. Cost-free gaps still contribute time. For a truly empty/unsupported beginning, use deterministic fun-friendly zero behavior, not Unix epoch, installation date guessed from an unrelated file, or an unlimited/zero divisor.

For clarity: the rolling **7/30-day cost numerator** retains its present exact trailing-hour membership, whereas the **new rate denominator is an explicitly labeled, day-normalized calendar view** anchored to the same current local date. This distinction avoids quietly changing all other AI Stats metrics or making a 7-day selection count eight full workdays due to boundary overlap. The goal is comparability and legibility, not a minute-by-minute elapsed-time expense meter.

**DST:** use local *calendar dates* for eligibility, but nominal **24h/date** and **8h/eligible date** in these displayed normalization rates, even on a real 23/25-hour daylight-saving-transition date. Preserve the actual source/timeline timestamps, current period inclusion and all other DST-sensitive metrics. Year/month/holiday rollovers must be deterministic for fixed `now`, zone and calendar, without depending on the browser's system timezone.

The hypothetical **Robot day** uses **the same selected-period C and active duration A** under every selector. Its per-shift value is not diluted by calendar hours or the number of workdays; its optional **W-shift period total** changes with the selected workday count. The 7/30-day underlying request membership and the separate AI Working Time metric remain unchanged.

1. Keep **the existing first `💸 AI Hourly Rate` card** with **three short labeled rows** and the original funny trait. Robot day imagines eight full hours at their active-hour burn rate.”** The user should understand that **Workday average** spreads *real-period cost* over scheduled hours, whereas Robot day **extrapolates running-time intensity**. Explain that the latter is hypothetical, never a historical invoice.
3. Where useful, include selected-period math in title/third-row accessible help, e.g. **840 Credits ÷ 168 calendar hours**, **840 ÷ 40 workday hours**, **840 ÷ 28 active hours × 8 = 240 Credits per robot day**, and **5 such days = 1,200 Credits**. Keep these explanatory quantities **in tooltip help**, not as a fourth headline number. Use the currently selected Credits/USD unit in displayed numbers and consistent help.
4. Preserve other cards' playful copy and title tooltips. Follow the existing `ContextHelp` hover/focus/touch/Escape behavior; the labels and values must be keyboard/screen-reader distinguishable. No partial/unavailable/observed diagnostics, technical provenance essay, pinning new help UI, or loss of normal responsive layout.
5. Three compact figures must wrap and remain legible in Classic/Web light/dark, narrow 360px/520px and increased text sizes while the peer cards in the same grid row keep their expected equal-height treatment. The robot's per-day unit and help must stay readable, not silently truncated.
