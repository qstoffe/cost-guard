bar, exact index/page counts immediately below and a separate source-backed work
line. Nodes and reserved slots stay fixed; identical detail updates coalesce and
screen readers receive meaningful phases rather than individual-root counts.

and redundant route notifications cannot erase pending/failed intent.

not transcripts or inferred inner-source stages. Existing source transport limits,
15-second local request deadline, three-failed-attempt guard and 45-second idle
coverage guard remain; currently owned bounded slow reads are not idle stalls.

qualification, deterministic ties, unknown-last behavior and stable 50-row epochs.
